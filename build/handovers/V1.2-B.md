## The headline: VD-7 standing conditions are real

design-vision decision #2 — "the verdict is a hypothesis; V1 populates
the conditions slots statically" — was never implemented:
`VerdictConditions.hypotheses` had been `[]` since P3-C01. New pure
engine module `src/engine/conditions.ts` derives deterministic bounds
from the policy's `kri_thresholds` (all six §3.7 dimensions as
green/amber/red band strings; `override_rate_pct` selects the tighter
high_risk band for High/Critical tiers using the FINAL post-override
tier) plus two graph pins (processing data zone pinned; max autonomy as
attested). Wired into `evaluate.ts`'s approved paths only — a rejection
carries no approval hypothesis. Formatter is defensive over the loose
`KriThresholds` index type: absent/unrecognized shapes emit nothing,
never an invented default (BC-V12B-02; the `green: 0` falsy-string trap
was reviewed and confirmed safe — `String(0)` is truthy).
