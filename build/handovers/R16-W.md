## Design review status — GVM DR-1 breach, flagged honestly
`build/prompts/R16-W.md` was written directly from a live walkthrough and
built from immediately — **not** from a cleared design review. The
`gvm-design-review` skill documents DR-1 as mandatory and blocking for any
chunk broader than a single function/config/doc change: "`/gvm-build`
refuses to start any build that is not backed by a cleared design review
… There is exactly one narrow waiver: chunks that touch only a single
function, a single config value, or a doc file." This chunk touches ~50
files across the flow, the summary, the confirmation step, the opening
screens and the verdict evidence model — well outside that waiver. R16-W
was therefore built in breach of DR-1, and is being **retroactively**
reviewed by design review 007, stated to be in progress at the time of
writing. I could not find a `design-review-007` artifact anywhere in the
repo (`design-review/` holds only `-001.html` through `-006.html`) to
independently confirm its status — recorded here as briefed, not verified.
