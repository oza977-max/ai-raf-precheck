Executive Summary

The GVM has been initialised for the AI Risk Appetite Pre-Check Engine — a tool that models AI use cases as data-flow graphs and evaluates them against a bank's machine-readable Risk Appetite Framework (RAF), returning: in/out of appetite, what invariant was violated, the binding constraint, and the minimal control set.
