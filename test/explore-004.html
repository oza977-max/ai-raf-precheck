D-002 — Critical

  • Given: a user completing the guided intake form who does not tick any of the six jurisdiction checkboxes (UK, US, EU, CA, SG, JP), none of which is ticked by default
  • When: they fill every other required field and look for the way forward
  • Then: 'Continue' enables with zero jurisdictions selected, and the flow runs to a full verdict. The completeness predicate tests the jurisdictions array for presence only, and an empty array is truthy. This finding supersedes charter 003's D-002, which claimed the form had no jurisdiction field. It does -- 003 was wrong. The defect is the missing guard, not a missing feature, and the user-facing consequence is the same: a verdict with no regulatory basis, produced silently.

Overall Assessment

This charter exists because charter 003 got things wrong, so the first duty is to say plainly what it got wrong. Three of its eight findings were artefacts of how it was driven rather than defects in the product. D-002 claimed the guided form has no jurisdiction field; it has six checkboxes, missed because the earlier walk enumerated only select elements and read a page dump that was truncated above them. D-005 and D-008 both rested on a browser carrying a previous session's data into what was assumed to be a first run; against cleared storage the demo seeds stamp the current policy version and the duplicate check counts correctly. Both are withdrawn. What survives is five defects re-confirmed by hand and one re-filed. The re-filed one matters most: the form does ask for jurisdiction, but nothing requires an answer, so a user can tick nothing, pass a completeness check that treats an empty array as filled, attest to a graph reading 'None specified', and receive a full verdict with no regulatory chain and no packs -- with nothing anywhere telling them the citations are missing or why. The consequence is what charter 003 described even though its cause was wrong. Alongside it, the product still discards the description it asks the user to write, and still asks a 2LoD reviewer to sign off on a page that does not show them the verdict. Those two are the same defect in different places -- reasoning the product produced, not shown to the person who needs it. The standing lesson is recorded as O-003: clear storage before a first-time-user tour, drive inputs with real keystrokes, and enumerate controls by DOM query rather than by reading rendered text. Every finding above was established that way.
