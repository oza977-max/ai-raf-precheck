Reachable Reasoning — pre-build gate (DR-1)

29 July 2026 · Full review · Strict criterion · 6 panels

Verdict: Build with caveats

Two Critical findings were raised and both are fixed in the specs before this verdict was issued, together with ten of the eleven Important findings. The verdict describes the amended design, not the design as first submitted.

The caveats that remain:

  • I-11 is recorded, not fixed. intake-draft.ts performs persistence I/O from src/components/, breaching cross-cutting §7. Pre-existing; R3-JU-7 knowingly builds on it. A future round should move draft persistence into src/store/.
  • P8-C06 grew. It now carries the verdict_id schema change, the policy prop path, the onCorrect contract change, and the exported helper. It was already the closing chunk; it is now the largest. Consider splitting it at build time if it exceeds one session.
  • No design round preceded this one. design-review-001.html carries no verdict string and no calibration row, so there is no prior design baseline to calibrate against. Findings here are R1-quality evidence despite the strict criterion.

Executive Summary

Six panels ran in parallel under a strict criterion: A Requirements Coverage, B Interface Contracts, C Structural Soundness, D Implementability, E Security, and one Panel F sub-panel dispatched from the ATAM utility tree's single (High importance, High risk) leaf — audit-write integrity under StrictMode.

15 unique findings: 2 Critical, 11 Important, 2 Minor. Capture-recapture across three cross-panel overlaps gives a Lincoln-Petersen estimate of roughly 11 against 15 unique found, so coverage sits above the 80% threshold and a second round is not indicated by the numbers.

The pattern worth naming. Both Criticals and two of the Importants (I-1, I-5) share one cause: a claim written into a spec without being checked against the code. In each case the check was one command. This is the third occurrence of that failure mode in this project's recent history — charter 003's D-002, the Phase 8 query-tightening premise, and now these four. The calibration mandate “verify documentation claims against behaviour, not against other documents” has now fired in two consecutive rounds and should be treated as a standing build check rather than a review note.

What held. ADR-EE-R3-1 was probed by two panels as possible scope creep and defended by both as design necessity, correctly disclosed in the chunk plan. Panel F confirmed VerdictDisplay has no effects at all, so mounting it on the sign-off page introduces no write path — the (H,H) scenario's risk is lower than the tree assumed. No vacuous tests were found in the 33 original cases.

C-1 — R3-RD-3 unimplementable — sign-off event cannot name its verdict Critical

Found by panel C, E (independent → elevated)

The twoloD_reviewed payload is {type, action, notes?}. R3-RD-3's fit criterion requires the audit event to refer to the verdict signed off, and TC-R3-RD-3-02 asserts it. No field can carry the reference. The schema already uses this pattern elsewhere (verdict_corrected.original_verdict_id, reasoning_trace_generated.verdict_id) — only this payload lacks it. Without it, a correction landing between page load and Approve means the attestation records nothing about which verdict was seen.

Disposition: Fixed. verdict_id added to the payload (verdict-audit §13.4), threaded from the rendered verdict, with a refusal where the verdict changed under the reviewer. TC-R3-RD-3-02 rewritten; TC-R3-RD-3-03 added for the race.

C-2 — The six-element list contradicts its own ADR Critical

Found by panel D

Element 4 (control evidence status) is sourced from PolicyFile.controls — today's policy. ADR-RL-R3-1 forbids showing anything recomputed against today's policy. Both were written into the same spec. RegisterDetailProps also has no policy field, so no path existed to supply it.

Disposition: Fixed. register-lifecycle §15.1a splits the two by scope: the verdict is historical, evidence status is current, and the split is argued rather than assumed — stale evidence status would tell a reviewer evidence exists when it may have lapsed. Prop path named. TC-R3-RD-7-01 pins the split.

I-1 — onCorrect required, its button ungated Important

Found by panel B, C

onCorrect: () => void is required and “Correct this classification?” renders unconditionally. §15.1's claim that the reclassification affordance is “not carried over” was unbuildable by reuse.

Disposition: Fixed. onCorrect becomes optional; button and reasoning-trace render only when supplied. TC-R3-RD-8-01 added.

I-2 — R3-JU-6 never traced to the sign-off surface Important

Disposition: Fixed. §15.1b extends the list; TC-R3-RD-6-01 added.

I-3 — Two unreconciled provisional mechanisms Important

Found by panel B

Disposition: Fixed. evaluation-engine §13.1a reconciles them as producer and label: caveats remain the per-rule detail, provisional_reasons is the sole determinant of status.

I-4 — findLatestVerdictEvent unexported Important

I-5 — TC-R3-NF-2-01's StrictMode rationale backwards Important

Found by panel F

Disposition: Fixed. Rationale corrected; the two opens are now described as two distinct properties, not one tested harder.

I-6 — XSS control implicit, never stated Important

Disposition: Fixed. verdict-audit §13.2a states the invariant and prohibits markdown/HTML renderers on pack-sourced fields.

I-8 — isComplete not enumerable; set-equality test needs an unbudgeted refactor Important
