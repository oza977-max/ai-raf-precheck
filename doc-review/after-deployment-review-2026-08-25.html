Critical Issues

Two findings that prevent publication:

False regulatory claim: EU AI Act high-risk regime live date

"EU AI Act high-risk regime went live 2 Aug 2026" was false at publication. The Digital Omnibus (Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, OJ 24 Jul 2026) postponed Annex III high-risk obligations to 2 Dec 2027 (Art. 50 transparency unchanged). This claim is load-bearing for Part 3 and anchors a critical regulatory narrative. Found by Panel B via live spot-check; independently re-verified by the orchestrating session. FIXED in 3rd ed. (correction disclosed inline).
